Section 10 of the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 — Judicial Separation.

⚖️
ONE-LINE MEANING

Separate, but still married.

Judicial separation means that a court allows a husband and wife to live separately, while their marriage itself continues.

🎓 Exam-ready answer

Section 10 of the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 provides for judicial separation. Either spouse may seek a decree on the statutory grounds referred to in the provision. Judicial separation permits the spouses to live separately without dissolving their marriage. Thus, the marital status continues, although the obligation of cohabitation is suspended.

06

Hard level: legal concepts.

For when you want to go beyond the basic definition.

What is a decree?

A decree is a formal judicial determination/order of a court. A decree of judicial separation is the court's formal grant of that matrimonial relief.

What is a petition?

A petition is a formal application presented to a court seeking a legal remedy.

What is cohabitation?

In matrimonial law, cohabitation generally refers to spouses living together as husband and wife.

Can judicial separation be rescinded?

Section 10 provides for rescission of the decree where the statutory conditions are satisfied and the court considers it just and reasonable.

Does judicial separation automatically become divorce?

No. Judicial separation does not itself dissolve the marriage. A later divorce requires a separate statutory basis and legal proceeding.

Can the spouses reconcile?

Yes. Reconciliation is possible. The legal consequences of reconciliation and the decree should be dealt with through the applicable legal process.
